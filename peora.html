The Process

Six stages between the aquifer and the seal.

Every jar, bottle and pouch passes through the same multi-barrier line before it leaves the plant.

01 — Source Intake
02 — Sediment Filtration
03 — Activated Carbon
04 — Reverse Osmosis
05 — UV Disinfection
06 — Ozonation & Seal
Reading
Groundwater drawn & logged
STAGE 01 / 06

Source intake

Groundwater is drawn from deep bore wells and logged before it enters the line — the starting point every batch is traceable back to.

STAGE 02 / 06

Sediment filtration

Multi-grade filters strip suspended solids and particulate matter, stage by stage, down to a fine micron level.

STAGE 03 / 06

Activated carbon

Carbon beds pull out residual chlorine, odour and organic compounds, clearing the way for the membrane stage.

STAGE 04 / 06

Reverse osmosis

Semi-permeable membranes bring dissolved solids down to a consistent, controlled range — batch after batch, no guesswork.

STAGE 05 / 06

UV disinfection

Ultraviolet exposure neutralises microbiological contaminants without adding a single chemical to the water.

STAGE 06 / 06

Ozonation & seal

Ozone keeps the water sterile right through packaging. Jars, bottles and pouches are filled and sealed the same day.
